2. WHY OPTICAL CAMERAS FAIL & WHY SONAR (SSS) IS USED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Optical Camera Limitations:
  In underwater environments (especially at depths > 20 meters), light attenuates rapidly, and water turbidity/particulates cause extreme scattering. Visual cameras cannot see beyond 2-5 meters.
* Side-Scan Sonar (SSS) Physics:
  Instead of light, SSS uses high-frequency acoustic waves (sound pulses) emitted sideways from a tow-fish or AUV.
* Acoustic Signature Components:
  1. Acoustic Backscatter (Highlight):
     High-density objects (metal hulls, hard plastics, rocks, concrete) reflect strong echoes, appearing as bright pixels.
  2. Acoustic Shadow:
     Sound cannot pass through solid objects, creating a zero-return black shadow zone directly behind the object.
     CRUCIAL WINNING INSIGHT:
     The length of the acoustic shadow, combined with the altitude of the sonar vehicle, allows calculating the EXACT physical height (vertical elevation) of the debris above the seabed!
  3. Slant-Range Distortion:
     Raw sonar measures travel time (slant range), which compresses near-nadir regions. Slant-range correction transforms the data into true horizontal ground range.
  4. Speckle Noise:
     Multi-path acoustic interference produces heavy grain/speckle noise requiring specialized filtering (Lee, Frost, or Bilateral filters).

4. COMPLETE 6-TIER TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Tier 1: Data Ingestion & Acoustic Preprocessing]
  - Input formats: Waterfall Sonar PNG/JPEG, GeoTIFF, raw XTF (eXtended Triton Format).
  - Preprocessing pipeline:
    * Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) for contrast stretching.
    * Bilateral / Lee Filter for speckle noise reduction while preserving sharp object edges.
    * Slant-range correction based on sonar altitude.

[Tier 2: Dual AI Detection & Segmentation Engine]
  - Primary Detector: Fine-tuned YOLOv8s / YOLOv11 (Fast, high mAP on acoustic highlights).
  - Secondary Segmenter: Segment Anything Model (SAM) or YOLOv8-Seg for irregular ghost net boundaries.
  - Model Export: ONNX runtime + TensorRT quantization for deployment on NVIDIA Jetson / Raspberry Pi 5.

[Tier 3: Acoustic Shadow Geometry & Elevation Calculation]
  - Formula: Object Height (H) = (Sonar Altitude * Shadow Length) / (Slant Range + Shadow Length).
  - Provides real-world hazard clearance clearance height for submarines and surface vessels.

[Tier 4: Temporal Persistence Engine]
  - Multi-pass coordinate matching. If an anomaly is detected at Lat/Long (x, y) across Pass A and Pass B (e.g. 2 hours apart), confidence increases to 99% (Confirmed Debris).

[Tier 5: Tactical Command Dashboard (Web UI)]
  - Waterfall Sonar Stream viewer with real-time sliding detection boxes.
  - Geospatial Sea Map (Leaflet.js / Mapbox) loaded with Bathymetry overlays.
  - Live trajectory plotting of AUV survey route in sensitive marine reserves (e.g., Gulf of Mannar).
  - Hazard severity classification: Critical (High entanglement danger) vs Moderate vs Low.

[Tier 6: Actionable Diver & Coast Guard Recovery Dispatch (PDF)]
  - Automated Generation of formal Recovery Mission Sheet:
    * Debris Incident ID & Timestamp
    * Exact GPS Coordinates (Lat / Long) & Estimated Depth
    * Debris Classification & Confidence Score
    * Estimated Volume & Dimensions
    * Recommended Recovery Equipment (Diver cutting shears, Crane grab, ROV robotic arm)
