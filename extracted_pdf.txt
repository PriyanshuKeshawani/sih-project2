RANK 1 — PS ID: 26014 | Integrated GIS Digital Public Infrastructure for Land Governance
(Land Stack)
Ministry / Organization: Ministry of Rural Development
Shortlist Hone Ka Reason: Sarkar ka direct National Priority project hai. GIS map wala demo
judges ko bahut impressed karta hai aur competitors kam hote hain.
1. Aasaan Samjh (What is the Problem?):
Zameen ke sabhi kaagaz (registry, tax, ownership, map) alag-alag oﬃces me bikhre hain. Aapko ek single map-based
website (Land Stack) banana hai jahan zameen par click karte hi uski saari jankari dikh jaye.
2. Aapko Kya Banana Hai (What to Build?):
Ek Google Maps jaisa dashboard jisme har zameen ka plot highlighted ho, aur click karne par owner name, land area,
registry history, aur tax status dikhe.
3. Beginners T ech Stack (Kaise Banayein?):
Frontend: Leaflet.js / Mapbox + React | Backend: Python FastAPI | Database: PostGIS / PostgreSQL
Judges Ke Saamne Presentation Tip:
PPT me ek working map dikhao jisme 1 plot click karke owner details load ho rahi hon.
